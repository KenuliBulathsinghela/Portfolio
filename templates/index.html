About

I am an undergraduate Information Systems student at the National Institute of Business Management (NIBM) with strong interests in data analytics, software development, and project management. I enjoy working with data to identify patterns, build dashboards, and develop predictive models that support informed decision making using tools such as Python, SQL, and Power BI. At the same time, I like building practical software solutions that solve real-world problems, including web and application systems such as booking platforms and management systems. Alongside technical development, I am also interested in Agile project management, where I contribute to planning projects, organizing tasks, managing sprint workflows, and coordinating teams using tools like Jira. By combining analytical thinking, development skills, and structured project coordination, I aim to contribute to building efficient, data-driven technology solutions.
