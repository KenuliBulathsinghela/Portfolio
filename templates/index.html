About

I’m an Information Technology for Business undergraduate at NIBM and a Business Analyst Intern at Sri Lanka Telecom (SLT), working at the intersection of business, data, and technology. I’m interested in understanding business problems, analyzing data, improving processes, and turning ideas into practical technology solutions. My experience spans Business Analysis, Data Analytics, Project Management, and Software Development, giving me a broad understanding of how technology can support real business needs. I have hands-on experience working with SQL, Power BI, Excel, Python, Tableau, Jira, and Confluence, along with experience in requirements analysis, BRDs, process analysis, Agile practices, stakeholder communication, and project coordination. Through academic and personal projects, I’ve worked on areas such as business intelligence dashboards, machine learning, data analysis, recommendation systems, software applications, and Agile project management. At SLT, I have also gained practical exposure to business analysis and project coordination within an enterprise environment. I’m particularly interested in opportunities where I can analyze problems, work with data, collaborate with teams, and help deliver technology-driven business solutions.
