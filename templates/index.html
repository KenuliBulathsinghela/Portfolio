About

I am an undergraduate Information Systems student at the National Institute of Business Management (NIBM) and currently working as a Business Analyst Intern at Sri Lanka Telecom (SLT), with strong interests in data analytics, software development, and project management. I enjoy working with data to identify patterns, build dashboards, and develop predictive models that support informed decision-making using tools such as Python, SQL, and Power BI. At the same time, I enjoy building practical software solutions that solve real-world problems, including web and application systems such as booking platforms and management systems. Alongside technical development, I am interested in Agile project management, where I contribute to project planning, task organization, sprint workflows, and team coordination using tools like Jira. By combining analytical thinking, development skills, business analysis, and structured project coordination, I aim to contribute to building efficient, data-driven technology solutions.
